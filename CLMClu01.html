What's at stake

The real cost of contracts
managed reactively

Contract risk is not an abstract concern. It shows up in missed renewals, regulatory penalties, and negotiations conducted without adequate information.

Financial Exposure
9.2% of annual revenue is the benchmark cost of poor CLM
Research consistently finds that organisations without systematic contract management lose nearly one tenth of annual revenue to missed obligations, auto-renewals at unfavourable rates, unclaimed credits, and unenforced penalty provisions. For a $500M business, that is $46M per year — largely invisible without structured tracking.
Compliance Risk
Regulatory obligations live inside contract language
Data processing agreements, business associate agreements, financial services schedules, and supplier compliance clauses embed specific regulatory obligations that must be actively monitored — not filed and forgotten. When a regulator asks whether your contractual data protection terms are being met, "we cannot locate that information" is not an adequate response.
Commercial Disadvantage
Counterparties with better information consistently negotiate better terms
Every contract negotiation is an information asymmetry problem. Counterparties who know their standard positions, their acceptable deviations, and the precedent in their existing portfolio negotiate from strength. Organisations without that intelligence routinely accept terms they do not need to accept — and have no way of knowing it.
