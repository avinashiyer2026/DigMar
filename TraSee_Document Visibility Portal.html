In TraSee, all client data lives in your Google Sheet — so to add a new matter, you add a row there directly.
How to add a new client:
1. Open your Google Sheet (link in setup)
2. Add a new row with: Client Name, Matter Type, Email, Phone, Deadline, Attorney, Notes
3. Add document names in columns H, J, L, N, P, R
4. Set their status in columns I, K, M, O, Q, S as Missing or Received
5. Click Refresh in TraSee
The next version of TraSee will include an in-app form that writes directly to your Sheet.
