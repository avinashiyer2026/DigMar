Our Evolution

From London litigation support to a global legal process outsourcing leader

1996

The Beginning

Founded in London as Law Copy by brothers Chris and Greg O'Reilly — pioneering 24/7/365 litigation support services.

2007

Technology Leadership

Early adopter of Relativity in UK/European markets, establishing expertise in market-leading ediscovery platforms.

2012

Global Expansion

Headquarters moved to Colorado, USA. Data centers established across Denver, Sydney, and Grand Cayman.

2017

India Operations

Chandigarh operations center opened with ISO 27001 certification, enabling scalable 24/7 legal outsourcing services delivery capabilities.

2021

Cyber Incident Response

Launched a dedicated cyber incident response support (CIRS) practice line supporting clients through data security breach response and forensic investigation.

2023

SOC 2 Certification

Achieved SOC 2 Type II certification for India operations center, reinforcing enterprise security commitment.

2026

Strategic Leadership

Jamie Berry appointed CEO of Global Legal Services, strengthening executive leadership across our global legal process outsourcing delivery model.
