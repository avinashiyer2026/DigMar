03 From the Field

PATTERN OBSERVED ACROSS LAW FIRM CONVERSATIONS

The Firms Struggling With eDiscovery Have One Thing in Common

Across Legalweek conversations and the broader pipeline of law firm engagements this quarter, one pattern emerged: the firms experiencing the most friction in their discovery programs were not under-tooled — they were under-governed.

They had AI. They had platforms. What they lacked was a consistent process for validating AI outputs, a defined escalation path for edge cases, and a review partner willing to take accountability for the end result — not just the hours billed.

The matters where LDM adds the most value are often the most time-pressured: a regulatory investigation with a hard agency deadline; a breach matter where the notification window is closing; litigation where production formats are non-negotiable and quality control is existential.

 

“The question isn’t whether to use AI in document review. It’s: who is accountable when the AI gets it wrong?”

— LDM’s position on defensible, attorney-governed AI review
