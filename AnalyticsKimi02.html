Data complexity costs
different functions in different ways.

The operational, legal, and strategic implications of unanalysed data vary by function. The following is written around those distinct pressures — not around what we sell.

Litigation & eDiscovery Teams

When document populations exceed practical review capacity, the cost of linear review becomes unsustainable. Without AI analytics, teams cannot prioritize relevance, eliminate redundancy, or identify hot documents efficiently. The result is either blown budgets or incomplete review — both failures with court and client consequences.

Chief Compliance Officers

Regulatory investigations and internal monitoring require the ability to detect patterns across communication archives, transaction records, and policy documents. Data estates that lack analytical structuring cannot reveal the anomalies, relationships, or risk indicators that compliance obligations demand.

Corporate Legal Operations

Contract portfolios, corporate records, and legacy matter files contain obligations, risks, and entitlements that remain invisible without systematic analysis. Organisations that cannot extract structured intelligence from these repositories make strategic decisions based on partial information.

Privacy & Data Breach Response

Breach notification obligations require rapid identification of personally identifiable information and protected health information across heterogeneous data sources. Manual review of breach datasets is neither timely nor accurate enough for regulatory compliance. AI analytics is the only scalable solution.

Review Cost

Linear review of large datasets is economically indefensible

When document populations exceed 100,000 records, linear review costs become disproportionate to matter value. AI analytics — including technology-assisted review, predictive coding, and conceptual clustering — reduce review populations by 40-70% while increasing accuracy. The refusal to deploy these tools constitutes a failure of professional diligence.

Strategic Blindness

Unanalysed contract and corporate data hides operational risk

Organisations with thousands of executed agreements, vendor contracts, and legacy corporate records cannot manually extract renewal dates, termination clauses, indemnification provisions, or compliance obligations. Without AI-powered contract analytics, these risks remain ungoverned until they crystallize into disputes or regulatory findings.

Compliance Velocity

Regulatory timelines exceed manual review capacity

Data subject access requests, regulatory investigations, and breach notification obligations carry strict deadlines. Manual review of the relevant datasets cannot meet these timeframes. AI analytics enable rapid identification of responsive material, PII/PHI detection, and privileged content segregation at velocities manual review cannot match.
