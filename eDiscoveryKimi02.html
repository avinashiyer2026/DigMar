Our Expertise

Comprehensive eDiscovery Solutions

From data collection to final production, we provide end-to-end eDiscovery services tailored to your case needs.

Data Collection & Forensic Preservation

Forensic data collections with on-site, remote, or secure lab capabilities. We ensure accurate evidence collection while maintaining chain-of-custody protocols. From mobile devices to enterprise systems, our process is seamless and defensible.

Data Processing, Hosting & Production

Using leading technology and adhering to local data privacy laws, we provide flexible processing and hosting solutions. Whether handling large-scale datasets or specialized productions, our team guides you 24/7 through every step.

Early Case Assessment (ECA)

We combine analytics, AI, and legal expertise to uncover key facts quickly. Our ECA solutions help you analyze risks, identify critical documents, and make informed decisions early—saving both time and costs.

Fact Investigation & Development

With advanced tools, we create clear data narratives including timelines, key players, and communication patterns. We consolidate data from diverse sources like timekeeper logs and expense reports for actionable insights.

Document Review Services

Scalable managed document review services that enhance your team's efficiency. From first-level review to privilege review and redactions, we apply AI-driven tools and expert workflows to reduce costs and meet deadlines.

Data Forensics & Investigations

Investigate incidents such as IP theft or unauthorized data access with our forensic expertise. We recover lost data, analyze suspicious activity, and determine if content was accessed or altered—ensuring full transparency.
