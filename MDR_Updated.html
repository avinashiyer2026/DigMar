What's at stake

The real cost of review done badly

Missed privilege calls, indefensible review protocols, and blown deadlines are not abstract risks. They carry concrete legal, regulatory, and financial consequences.

Legal Risk
Privilege waiver is irreversible
An inadvertent production of privileged material — through inadequate review protocols or undertrained reviewers — can result in subject-matter waiver that cannot be clawed back. The review protocol is your first line of defence.
Regulatory Exposure
Regulators judge the process, not just the output
Enforcement bodies reviewing document productions look at whether the methodology was proportionate, documented, and defensible — not only at whether responsive documents were included.
Financial Impact
Linear review is one of litigation's most controllable costs
Technology-assisted review, properly deployed, consistently reduces review cost by 30–40%. Organisations that rely on linear review at scale are absorbing unnecessary cost that is entirely avoidable.

Data security & physical controls

A review environment that
meets enterprise security standards

For legal and compliance teams, the security of the review environment is a due diligence requirement — not a preference. Our controls are designed to satisfy that scrutiny.

Secure, Monitored Facilities Only
All review takes place in controlled, monitored environments. No remote or work-from-home review — the physical perimeter is enforced at every site.
No Mobile Devices on the Review Floor
Mobile devices are prohibited in all review areas. This eliminates the primary vector for inadvertent data exfiltration at the physical layer.
Client-Dedicated Project Rooms
Each client engagement operates in a physically segregated workspace. Matter materials, credentials, and review data are never co-mingled across clients.
AES-256 Encryption in Transit & at Rest
All data is encrypted throughout its lifecycle — from ingestion through review to delivery. Chain-of-custody documentation is maintained for every transfer.
No Screenshots & No Camera Access
Screenshots are technically blocked and camera access is prohibited in all review areas — preventing capture of on-screen data by any means.
ISO 27001 & SOC 2 Type II Certified
Independent audit of our information security management system, renewed annually. Certifications are available for due diligence review upon engagement.
24/7 Physical & Cyber Monitoring
Facilities are monitored continuously with advanced surveillance. Security operations are active around the clock across all global locations.
Mandatory Data Security Training
All reviewers undergo rigorous security training before accessing any matter. Low attrition means a trained, consistent workforce — not a revolving door of temporary staff.
GDPR, HIPAA & Multi-Framework Compliance
Operational compliance across all major data protection regimes. Relevant frameworks are identified and applied at the matter intake stage — not added as an afterthought.
